HOW TO PLAY

Basics — Both pick 1–6 simultaneously. Same = WICKET. Different = runs (batsman's number).

Modes

  • Normal — standard match with overs & wickets
  • Super Over — 1 over, 1 wicket, tiebreaker format, no powers
  • Draft — ban 2 numbers before the match; those numbers are locked for you

Batsman Powers (4, once per innings each):

  • 🛡 Shield — survive one wicket
  • ✖2 Doubler — double runs this ball
  • 💣 Blitz — +3 bonus runs
  • 🃏 Wildcard — random number for you

Bowler Powers (4, once per innings each):

  • ❄️ Freeze — opponent must repeat last number
  • 🔮 Predict — next wicket dismisses TWO batters
  • ⚡ Surge — sixes = 12 for batsman's next 3 balls (bowler's weapon)
  • 🧱 Wall — batsman capped at 3 this ball

Tournament — round-robin group stage → semis → final. Tied matches → Super Over.

Rain/DLS — 30% chance in 10+ over matches. Target recalculated automatically.
